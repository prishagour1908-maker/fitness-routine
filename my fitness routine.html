Mt Fitness Routine

A balanced fitness routine combines strength training and cardio. Start with a 3-day split (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday). Perform full-body movements like squats, push-ups, lunges, and dumbbell rows. Add 30 minutes of moderate cardio or active recovery on the alternate days.

Do this single routine Monday through Friday for a complete blend of strength and cardio.



Essential Weekly Template
Part 1: Warm-Up (3 Minutes)
Minute 1: March in place.
Minute 2: Gentle arm circles.
Minute 3: Light torso twists.

Part 2: Strength & Cardio Circuit (12 Minutes)
Do each exercise for 40 seconds, then rest for 20 seconds. Repeat the entire list 3 times.
Exercise 1: Sit-to-stands from a chair.
Exercise 2: Wall or counter-top push-ups.
Exercise 3: Shadowboxing (punching the air to raise your heart rate).
Exercise 4: Plank hold on your knees or toes.

Part 3: Cool-Down (5 Minutes)
Minutes 16-18: Slow, gentle marching in place.
Minutes 19-20: Deep breathing while stretching your arms and legs.


Some daily action plans
Strength Days (Mon / Wed / Fri)
Do each movement for 40 seconds. Rest for 20 seconds. Repeat the whole circuit 3 times total.
Minutes 1-5: Easy arm circles and high knees to warm up. Minute 6: Sit-to-stands (using a household chair). Minute 7: Wall push-ups or counter-top push-ups. Minute 8: Alternating backward steps (reverse lunges). Minute 9: Plank hold (on knees or toes). Minutes 10-15: Repeat the circuit two more times.

Cardio Days (Tue / Thu)
Minutes 1-3: Walk at a normal, easy pace.   Minutes 4-17: Walk fast enough to make breathing heavy. Minutes 18-20: Slow down to a gentle stroll.
